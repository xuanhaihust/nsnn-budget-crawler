# 2026-09-15 — intra-year cash flow: what this corpus can and cannot answer

The owner's research goal for this dataset is the **periodicity of money moving in and out of
the State Treasury** — the flows that enter and leave the money market — in order to anticipate
their scale, frequency and timing. Of the four treasury flows named, this crawl covers exactly
one: *budget disbursement and collection from recurrent spending, development investment, and
other revenue-expenditure*.

The literal request was a table and chart of **national** revenue and spending **by month**.

## Two premises fail against the corpus. Both were measured, not assumed.

**1. There is no monthly data anywhere in the corpus.**

```sql
SELECT kind, COUNT(*) FROM dim_period GROUP BY kind;
```

`dim_period.kind` takes exactly four values — `Quý` (and only ever `Quý I`), `6 tháng`,
`9 tháng`, `Năm`. Not one of the 3,814,427 rows is a month. The Ministry's disclosure forms
publish **cumulative year-to-date** figures at four points, and that is the finest time
resolution the source has. No amount of parsing produces a month from it.

**2. It is not national.**

This is 34 provinces' **local** budgets. No central-government budget, no bond issuance or
maturity, no treasury deposits at commercial banks, no OMO. No province publishes a national
figure, and adding provinces up does **not** yield one — the set of reporting provinces changes
from year to year, and the Ministry layer is only part of the disclosure regime (see the scope
limit in `CLAUDE.md`).

So the request as phrased cannot be answered from this source. What follows is the deepest
granularity it does support.

## What was built instead: quarterly flows, differenced from the cumulative points

`dashboard/aggregate.py` gained `quarters()` and `profile()`. For each (province, year) that
publishes all four cumulative points on the `ƯỚC THỰC HIỆN QUÝ` column, the four quarterly
**flows** are the successive differences, and each is expressed as a **share of that year's
total**.

Share, not sum, and the reason matters: the set of provinces reporting changes every year, so a
sum moves with coverage and a share does not. A coverage-robust statistic is the only kind that
can be compared across years here.

| | form | indicator |
|---|---|---|
| revenue | `B60` | `A TỔNG THU NSNN TRÊN ĐỊA BÀN` |
| spending | `B61` | `TỔNG CHI NSĐP` |

### The cumulative premise was checked, not assumed

**73 of 80** (province, year) sets satisfy `Quý I ≤ 6 tháng ≤ 9 tháng` — 91.2%. The series is
cumulative.

### The result

Median share of the year landing in each quarter:

| | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | n |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Revenue | 22.8% | 23.8% | 23.1% | **28.2%** | 55 |
| Spending | 20.2% | 21.3% | 20.4% | **35.2%** | 57 |

The four shares do not sum to exactly 100%, and should not be made to. Each quarter's median
is taken **independently** across provinces, so four medians carry no constraint to add up;
observed sums run 94.3%–109.9% by year (overall: revenue 98.0%, spending 97.1%). Normalising
them to 100% would be correcting the numbers, so they are left as computed.

**Spending is back-loaded; revenue is not.** Q4 takes 35.2% of the year's spending against
roughly 20% in each earlier quarter, and this is stable across all six years in the data
(31.6%–42.2%), so it is not one year's noise. Revenue's Q4 share is 28.2% — barely above an
even 25%.

For the money-market question that motivated this: through Q1–Q3 the treasury **absorbs** more
than it releases, and in Q4 that reverses sharply. That asymmetry, not the level, is the
seasonal signal.

### Sample size and exclusions

| | revenue | spending |
|---|---:|---:|
| (province, year) sets seen | 141 | 142 |
| with all four cumulative points | 67 | 69 |
| dropped for a negative quarter | 12 | 12 |
| **used** | **55** | **57** |

A "negative quarter" means the cumulative series **falls** — Gia Lai 2023 reports 9 months as 0
after 2.9 nghìn tỷ at 6 months; Đà Nẵng 2018 reports 6 months above 9 months. That is a source
defect. Those sets are **dropped outright**, never clamped to zero and never interpolated:
inferring what the source meant is the owner's call, not the code's.

## What this does not cover, and where it is

The other three treasury flows the owner named are not in the Ministry's budget disclosure
portal at all, so no crawler over this source can reach them:

| needed | published by |
|---|---|
| national revenue/spending **by month** | Ministry of Finance monthly estimates; General Statistics Office |
| treasury cash balance, deposits at commercial banks | State Treasury / SBV reporting |
| government bond issuance and maturity | HNX auction calendar and results |
| repo / reverse-repo of valuable papers (OMO) | SBV, per session |
